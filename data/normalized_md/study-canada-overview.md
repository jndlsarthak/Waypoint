### Changing schools

As of November 8, 2024, you can no longer change schools on the same study permit. If you want to change schools, you need to get a new study permit by applying to extend your current one. [Learn more about changing schools](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/change-schools.html).
